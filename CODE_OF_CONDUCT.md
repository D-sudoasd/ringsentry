# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

RingSentry contributors and maintainers are expected to make participation
welcoming and free from harassment, regardless of age, body size, disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, colour,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected conduct

Examples of constructive participation include:

- using respectful, precise language;
- accepting technical feedback without personal attacks;
- documenting scientific assumptions and uncertainty;
- respecting privacy, unpublished research, and beamline data restrictions;
- focusing disagreement on reproducible evidence and project scope; and
- acknowledging mistakes and helping repair their effects.

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language,
sexualized attention, deliberate disclosure of private information, sustained
disruption, or retaliation against someone who reports a concern.

## Scope

This code applies in the repository, issue tracker, pull requests, review
discussions, and project-related public or private communication. It also
applies when someone officially represents the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not place sensitive conduct reports in a public issue. Contact the
maintainer privately through the contact information exposed on the
maintainer's GitHub profile or ORCID-linked academic profile. If no private
route is available, open a minimal issue requesting private contact without
including names, evidence, or identifying details.

The maintainer will assess reports in context, protect confidentiality as far
as practical, and may edit or remove content, issue a warning, restrict
participation, or permanently exclude a participant. Decisions and any public
explanation will minimize disclosure of the people involved.
